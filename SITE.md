# Site Vision: Healthy + Brain

**Project Type:** Biological sovereignty and longevity ecosystem
**Stitch Project ID:** 1336072952115328009 (from DESIGN.md)

## 1. Vision

"Healthy + Brain" is a high-performance biometric tracking platform that merges clinical precision with a premium, sleek interaction layer. Users can track their biological metrics (HRV, Steps, Glucose), earn "Delayed Dopamine" tokens for healthy habits, and access AI-powered nutrition coaching.

## 2. Target Users

- Health-conscious individuals seeking biological optimization
- Biohackers interested in longevity and performance tracking
- Users who want to gamify their health journey with rewards

## 3. Core Features

- **Biometric Dashboard:** Real-time HRV, Steps, Glucose tracking
- **Bio-Economy:** Token system rewarding delayed gratification
- **Social Synergy:** Community challenges combining cardio + strength
- **AI Nutrition:** Plate scanning with macro calculation
- **Focus Sessions:** Pomodoro-style deep work with biometric feedback
- **Sleep Optimization:** Circadian rhythm tracking and sunlight sync
- **Achievement System:** Badges and milestones for engagement
