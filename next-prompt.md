A modern, high-converting landing page for the Healthy + Brain biological sovereignty platform. The page should showcase the core value proposition: biometric tracking, AI nutrition, and gamified health optimization.

# Design System: healthy + brain

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The design system for "Healthy + Brain" is a high-performance, biological sovereignty focused aesthetic. It merges the clinical precision of biometric tracking with a premium, sleek interaction layer. The "vibe" is dark, immersive, and high-tech (biopunk-lite), using deep noir backgrounds to make vibrant metabolism-related accents pop.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Space Obsidian (#0a0a0a):** Primary background for immersion and high contrast.
- **Surface Glass Noir (#121212 / rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.7)):** Secondary background for cards and overlays, using glassmorphism.
- **Bio-Primary Orange (#ff8a00):** Used for critical calls to action, metabolic alerts, and "hot" state feedback.
- **Neuro-Primary Blue (#00d1ff):** Used for calming effects, focus sessions, and hydration/rest metrics.
- **Fluorescent Bio-Green (#13ec5b):** Project-wide accent for growth, positive trends, and "System OK" states.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Inter:** The core font family throughout the app.
- **Weights:** Bold (700+) for headers to establish biological hierarchy; Medium (500) for labels; Regular (400) for body data.
- **Character:** Low letter-spacing to feel compact and data-rich.

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:**
  - **Glow Buttons:** Pill-shaped with a subtle outer glow matching the accent color.
  - **Ghost Buttons:** Transparent background with vibrant borders.
- **Cards/Containers:**
  - **Glassmorphism:** Generous corner roundness (24px+), blurred background (12px blur), and thin high-contrast borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)).
- **Inputs/Forms:**
  - **High-Contrast:** Dark inputs that flip to pure white/black contrast on focus, providing an unmistakable "active" state.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Density:** High-density data visualization to minimize scrolling and maximize insight.
- **Spacing:** Tight margins between related biometric data; large breathing room between major physiological sections.
- **Gamification:** Integrated "XP" and progress bars using neon gradients to drive user completion.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Hero Section:** Bold headline about biological sovereignty, subheadline about biometric optimization, CTA button with glow effect
2. **Features Grid:** 3-4 key features (Bio-Tracking, AI Nutrition, Bio-Economy, Social Synergy) with icons
3. **Stats/Metrics Preview:** Show example biometric data visualization (HRV, Steps, Glucose)
4. **CTA Section:** Final call-to-action to start the journey
5. **Footer:** Minimal with links to main app sections
